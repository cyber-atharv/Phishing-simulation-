🛡️ Security Countermeasures & Best Practices
Recommended proactive defense strategies
🔍

1. Inspect & Scrutinize

Check sender email headers, exact domain spellings, TLS certificates, and grammar before clicking any attachment or button.

🛑

2. Stop & Reflect

Never allow artificial urgency, fear of penalty, or executive authority pressure to dictate immediate password entry.

🔐

3. Enforce Multi-Factor (MFA)

Adopt hardware security keys (FIDO2 / WebAuthn) or authenticator apps (Aegis, Google Authenticator) rather than SMS.
