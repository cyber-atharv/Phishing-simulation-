Legal Framework — IT Act 2000 (India)

Relevant sections of the Information Technology Act, 2000 that apply to phishing and identity theft offenses.

Section 66C

Identity Theft — Whoever fraudulently or dishonestly makes use of the electronic signature, password or any other unique identification feature of any other person.

Punishment: Up to 3 years imprisonment + Fine up to ₹1 Lakh

Section 66D

Cheating by Personation — Whoever cheats by personation by using any communication device or computer resource.

Punishment: Up to 3 years imprisonment + Fine up to ₹1 Lakh

Section 43

Unauthorized Access — Penalty and compensation for damage to computer, computer system, etc. without permission of the owner.

Compensation: Up to ₹5 Crore as decided by Adjudicating Officer
