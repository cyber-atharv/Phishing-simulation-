Here is the automated killchain cybercriminals execute immediately after harvesting credentials:

1

Credential Stuffing (Botnets)

Because 65% of people reuse passwords, automated bots immediately test your username/password across 500+ top services (banks, PayPal, Amazon, Netflix, corporate VPNs).

2

Dark Web Monetization

Your credentials and session telemetry are bundled and sold on illicit brokerages (like Genesis Market or Russian Market) for as little as $2 to $15.

3

Account Takeover (ATO) & Session Hijacking

Attackers log in, change the recovery email, reset backup phone numbers, generate new 2FA codes, and lock you out permanently.

4

Lateral Spear-Phishing

Your compromised account is used to email all your friends, family, and coworkers with urgent malicious links, exploiting existing interpersonal trust.

Emergency Incident Response Checklist

If you ever enter credentials into a suspicious website in real life, perform these steps immediately:

1. Change Password Immediately

Change the password on the legitimate website first, and on ANY other site where you used that same password.

2. Revoke Active Sessions

Go to Security Settings and click "Log out of all other devices / active sessions" to kick out attackers.

3. Enable Hardware/App MFA (2FA)

Use Authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Aegis) or FIDO2 YubiKeys rather than SMS verification.

4. Inspect Account Settings

Check for unauthorized email forwarding rules, linked third-party apps, or changed recovery phone numbers.
