🛡️ How to Protect Against Quishing:

🔍 Inspect Before Scanning Never scan QR codes from unknown emails or messages. If it claims to be from your IT dept, call them directly.
🔗 Preview the URL Use your phone's QR scanner to preview the URL before opening. Check if the domain looks legitimate.
🚫 Don't Trust Urgency Legitimate organizations don't threaten account deletion via QR codes. Urgency = red flag.
📞 Verify Independently If an email asks you to take immediate action, contact the sender through official channels first.
