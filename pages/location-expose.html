Why This Matters

Every time you click a suspicious link, the attacker's server can silently log all of the above information. Combined with phishing, this data can be used for:

Targeted Attacks
Using your location and device info to craft personalized spear-phishing messages.
Session Hijacking
Stealing browser fingerprints to impersonate your device on other services.
Physical Stalking
Knowing your approximate location can put your physical safety at risk.
Identity Fraud
Combining leaked data with stolen credentials for identity theft and financial fraud.

How to Protect Yourself

Use a VPN to mask your real IP address and location
Use privacy-focused browsers (Brave, Firefox with hardened settings)
Never click suspicious links — always verify URLs before visiting
Disable location services for your browser when not needed
Use browser extensions like uBlock Origin and Privacy Badger
Keep your browser updated to patch known vulnerabilities
